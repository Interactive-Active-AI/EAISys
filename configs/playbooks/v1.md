# EAISys Zero-Privilege Operational Playbook (v1)

Concise, embodiment-agnostic & URDF-morphology-aware lessons distilled across manipulation episodes.
Every rule strictly respects **Zero Simulator Privilege** (raw multi-view RGB + hardware encoders only).

## [universal] Visual-Servoing & Contact Discipline
- Cross-verify 3D alignment across ALL active camera views before descending into contact; never rely on a single view for depth.
- Use `STEP_COARSE` (`0.04 m`) for free-space transit, switch to `STEP_FINE` (`0.02 m`) near the target, and use `STEP_ULTRA_FINE` (`0.01 m`) for tight insertion, rim clearance, or precision placement.
- After `GRASP` + `MV_UP`, visually confirm the object lifted in the side/front view and check the raw jaw encoder; if the grasp slipped, `RELEASE`, realign visually, and retry.
- If `joint_tracking_max_err_rad` spikes or TCP stalls across consecutive units, you are likely colliding with a wall, rim, or handle—retract (`MV_UP` or opposite axis) before re-approaching.

## [articulated] Doors, Handles & Hinged Mechanisms
- Inspect the clearance behind the handle in side and wrist views before lowering; wide grippers may require `ROT_YAW_*` or approaching slightly off-center before hooking behind the bar.
- Pull along the handle's sweep arc in short chunks while monitoring hinge rotation visually in the front/side view.

## [sorting] Walled Bins & Multi-Compartment Organizers
- Lift well above bin divider walls before translating laterally across compartments; confirm clear air gap underneath the held object in the front/side view.
- Align over the target silhouette compartment in the overhead view before descending and emitting `RELEASE`.

## [insertion] Rings, Pegs & Precision Assembly
- Grasp handles/tabs symmetrically near the ring edge so the inner bore stays unobstructed in the overhead and wrist views.
- Center the ring bore directly concentric with the peg tip in BOTH overhead and side views using `STEP_ULTRA_FINE` before descending slowly and releasing.

## [wiping] Non-Prehensile Continuous Surface Contact
- Do NOT sweep while hovering in mid-air: keep descending (`MV_DOWN`) until you see BOTH a vertical stall in URDF FK TCP Z (a commanded `MV_DOWN` moves significantly less than the current step size, confirming real tabletop contact) AND brown dirt marks visibly disappearing under the pad in CAM 1 / CAM 3.
- If a horizontal sweep across the stain erases ZERO visible dirt in CAM 1, you are still hovering above the table—immediately emit `MV_DOWN` before sweeping again.
- Once contact is confirmed and marks are erasing, sweep smoothly across all remaining brown patches in overlapping strokes until the white tabletop is completely spotless in CAM 1.

## [bimanual] Coordinated Dual-Arm Manipulation
- Approach opposing handles symmetrically in parallel (`left_chunk` and `right_chunk`) so neither arm knocks the object out of the partner's reach.
- Parallel linkage grippers retract slightly upward as the jaws close: descend until the bottom tips of the open fingers clearly extend slightly below the bottom edge of each handle bar in the side elevation view (so the closed flat pads straddle the full bar thickness) before issuing `GRASP`.
- Lift smoothly with synchronized upward `MV_UP` chunks well clear above the tabletop (creating a large, unmistakable air gap underneath the entire object in the side view before considering `DONE`); if either gripper slips (jaw encoder closes fully to empty air), `RELEASE`, descend slightly deeper, and re-grasp.
